Jack's Compass

A compass, a spirit level, and coordinates — a small Android utility that needs no sign-up.

What the app does

  • Compass: shows magnetic heading, with your current latitude and longitude listed above the dial.
  • Level: uses the accelerometer to show tilt in the horizontal and vertical axes.
  • No account, no sign-up, no in-app purchases. Those are the only three tools in the app.
